ABOUT THE PROJECT

Helping employees work smarter

The AI Workplace Productivity Assistant was developed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence and prompt engineering can be applied to everyday workplace tasks.

The solution focuses on reducing repetitive work while keeping humans responsible for reviewing and approving AI-generated information.

RESPONSIBLE AI

AI should assist people, not replace judgement

🔐 Privacy

Users should avoid entering confidential or sensitive workplace information into AI systems.

✓ Human Review

AI-generated content should be reviewed by a person before important decisions or communications.

⚖️ Bias & Accuracy

AI can produce inaccurate or biased information. Important information should therefore be verified.

🧠 Human Oversight

The assistant supports workplace productivity but does not replace human responsibility or judgement.
